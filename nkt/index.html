Three reasons the market is too cautious

Model and valuation: Daniela

1. Demand is already contracted

The Transmission backlog of EUR 11.6bn covers 97% of the revenue I forecast to 2031, and 118% with the TenneT framework agreement. 70% is interconnectors, which governments buy for security of supply.

2. Margins have further to go

Transmission margin rose from 15.1% (2025) to 20.2% (Q2-26). I forecast 25% by 2029; my reverse DCF shows today's price implies only about 20.5%.

3. The cash arrives in 2028

Capex falls from EUR 695m (2025) to EUR 280m (2028E), and free cash flow swings from EUR −491m (2026E) to +476m (2029E). My 2027-28 EBITDA sits 4-7% below consensus, so NKT does not need to surprise.

When I would change my mind: if the Transmission margin falls back below 19%, or if a whole year goes by without a major new order.

Catalysts

Q3 results (19 Nov 2026), conversion of TenneT's >EUR 2.5bn framework into backlog, new capacity starting up during 2027 (Karlskrona, Cologne, NKT Eleonora), 2027 capex guidance, and the Czech and Slovak antitrust rulings.

Risks I flagged

Competitors adding capacity, cost overruns on fixed-price projects, delays to new plants and the NKT Eleonora, and an outflow of customer prepayments.
