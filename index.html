Stock News Agent

AUTOMATION · 2026
Problem
Following news for 21 holdings by hand is slow, and most headlines are noise: repeated stories, "stocks to buy" lists and price predictions.
What I built
An AI agent that runs every evening. It searches news for each stock, keeps only material events (earnings, rating changes, contracts, M&A), checks every number on the source page and emails me a digest with a sentiment tag and a link for each item.
Result
One short email each evening instead of searching stock by stock. On its first run it checked 21 stocks, reported 8 with material news and set aside 13 with nothing relevant.
Tools
ClaudeScheduled routineGmail
View code and sample digest
Cross-validated ROC-AUC by model (higher is better) Axis starts at 0.85. The tuned Gradient Boosting model scored 0.92 on unseen test data.

Credit Card Fraud Detection

ANALYTICS · LBS 2026
Problem
Only 0.59% of 671,028 card transactions are fraud, yet they make up 4–5% of the money moved. A model must find the rare cases without flagging every customer.
My part
Team project for the LBS Data Science for Business course. I owned the exploratory analysis, the descriptive statistics and the machine-learning section: feature engineering, model comparison and tuning.
What AI did
Claude Code turned the notebook into a live Streamlit dashboard, following a build plan I reviewed and approved first.
Result
ROC-AUC of 0.92 on 134,206 unseen transactions. Fraudulent payments average $527 against $68 for legitimate ones, and night-time and amount are the strongest signals.

Avocado Market Dashboard

ANALYTICS · LBS 2026
Problem
A grocery chain with 500+ US stores needs to decide how many avocados to buy, what to charge and where to grow.
My part
Individual project. I did the analysis across 59 US regions: prices, seasonality, organic vs conventional, bag sizes, regional growth and price elasticity with a log-log regression.
What AI did
Claude Code turned my notebook into a five-tab interactive web app, after I approved its build plan. The analysis logic is identical to my notebook.
Result
Three actions: raise prices in peak season and promote in February, add distribution in fast-growing Southeast cities, and drop extra-large bags where they barely sell.
