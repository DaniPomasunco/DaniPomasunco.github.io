How I work with Claude

The judgement is mine

I set the question, choose the data and the method, and decide what the numbers mean. Valuations, models and conclusions are my own.

Claude is my support

It builds the tools from my work, runs the routines I design, and argues against my thesis so I find the weak spots first. I approve its plan before it writes anything.

It has to be usable

Each project ends as something people can open: an interactive page or a process that runs on its own, not a file on my laptop.
