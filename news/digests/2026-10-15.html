RKLB · Rocket Lab ▼ Negative
After Wednesday's close, the company confirmed that Neutron's first flight slips from the fourth quarter of 2026 to 2027, because of a defect in the composite dome of the first-stage tank; it says it has already identified the fix. The stock fell 7.9% on Wednesday to $59.18 and today traded around $50–53 (10% to 15% lower). Cantor Fitzgerald keeps Overweight with a $122 price target and sees the delay as disappointing but not serious in the long run; it now expects the first launch in the second quarter of 2027.
