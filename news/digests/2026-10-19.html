NVAX · Novavax ● Neutral
According to an SEC filing on 16 October, Shah Capital sold about 2.87 million shares at an average of $11.35 (about $32.6 million) and keeps 6.46%. It also stops being an activist shareholder: it will no longer seek to influence control of the company, after pushing for a sale in 2025 and opposing the board at the 2026 AGM. Less pressure to sell the company, but also a large shareholder cutting its stake.

NIO · NIO Inc. ▲ Positive
According to figures cited by its president, NIO sold 111,638 vehicles in China in the third quarter, ahead of BMW (107,210), Mercedes-Benz (99,592) and Audi (94,597). It is the first time it has beaten each of the three German brands. The stock was up nearly 1% before the open, though it is down 33% for the year.
