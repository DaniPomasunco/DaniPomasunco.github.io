BMBL · Bumble ▲ Positive
Ithaca Holdings agreed to buy Bumble for about US$1.3bn, at US$6.05 per share in cash, a 122% premium to the price before the sale became known. Blackstone and founder Whitney Wolfe Herd have already committed their votes in favour, and Ithaca's co-founders will become co-CEOs. The stock rises 56% to US$5.94, close to the offer price: if you hold BMBL, little upside is left and the stock will be delisted when the deal closes.
