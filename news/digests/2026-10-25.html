CPNG · Coupang ▼ Negative
The chairman of Korea's Fair Trade Commission (KFTC), Joo Byung-ki, said today on KBS television that Coupang's market power is "like a lethal weapon" against small merchants and that the company is "no longer a lightweight". He proposed tougher fines, for example up to 1% of total revenue, although this is a policy proposal, not an announced sanction. The KFTC has already sent a report recommending that Coupang be fined for refusing an on-site inspection in August, related to passing the cost of coupons on to suppliers. That case is still under deliberation.
