My work

  • Chose the watchlist and what counts as news: earnings and guidance, analyst rating changes, deals, contracts, regulation
  • Wrote the rules for what to drop: repeats, "stocks to buy" lists, price predictions, promotional pieces
  • Read the first weeks of output and redesigned it: ticker-only searches often came back empty, so I added macro, industry, peer and regulation news with how each one affects my stocks

How I used Claude

  • Automate: a scheduled Claude routine searches the web, checks sources and writes the email, every evening
  • Build: Claude drafted the instructions with me and built this archive page
  • It summarises; it never recommends trades. I check the source before I act

How a night works

  1. At 17:52 the routine starts and loads my watchlist and a map of what moves each stock (rates, oil, peers, regulators, countries)
  2. It searches the last 24 hours of news for each company and for those drivers
  3. It drops repeats and promotional pieces, and opens the source page to take every number from it
  4. It emails me: the top stories, news by stock, context that affects my stocks, and the coming calendar
